COMPANY IT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Hardware Support Policy

Document ID: IT-HW-001
Department: IT Support

Issue: Laptop or desktop hardware problem

Recommended troubleshooting:

1. Identify the affected device.
2. Check whether the device powers on.
3. Check the power cable or charger.
4. Restart the device if possible.
5. Record any error message shown.
6. If the problem continues, create a hardware support ticket.

Escalation:
Physical damage, overheating, smoke, burning smell, or electrical safety concerns should be escalated immediately to IT Support.

Source: Company Hardware Support Policy, IT-HW-001
