Email Troubleshooting Guide

Document ID: IT-EMAIL-001
Department: IT Support

Issue: Outlook is not syncing emails

Recommended troubleshooting:

1. Check that the employee has an active internet connection.
2. Check whether Outlook shows "Disconnected" or "Trying to connect".
3. Make sure "Work Offline" is disabled.
4. Use Send/Receive > Send/Receive All Folders.
5. Restart Outlook.
6. If the issue continues, check Outlook Web Access to determine whether the problem is with the desktop application or the mailbox.
7. If the issue cannot be resolved through these steps, create an IT support ticket.

Escalation:
If the employee reports suspicious emails, unauthorized account activity, or possible account compromise, stop normal troubleshooting and escalate the issue to IT Security.
