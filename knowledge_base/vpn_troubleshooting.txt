VPN Troubleshooting Guide

Document ID: IT-VPN-001
Department: IT Support

Issue: Company VPN is not connecting

Recommended troubleshooting:

1. Check the employee's internet connection.
2. Confirm that the employee is using the approved company VPN application.
3. Verify the employee's username and password.
4. Restart the VPN application.
5. Restart the computer if the VPN still does not connect.
6. Check whether other company services are accessible.
7. If the VPN continues to fail, create an IT support ticket.

Escalation:
If the employee reports unauthorized access, stolen credentials, suspicious login activity, or possible security compromise, escalate immediately to IT Security.
