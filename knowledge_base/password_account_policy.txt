COMPANY IT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Password and Account Policy

Document ID: IT-AUTH-001
Department: IT Security

Password guidance:

1. Employees must never share their company password with another person.
2. Employees should use the approved password-reset process when they forget their password.
3. Employees should not enter company credentials into suspicious websites or links.
4. Suspected credential compromise must be reported to IT Security immediately.

Escalation:
Any report involving a shared password, stolen credentials, suspicious login, phishing, or possible account compromise must be escalated to IT Security.

Priority:
Security-related incidents should be treated as high-priority tickets.

Source: Company Password and Account Policy, IT-AUTH-001
